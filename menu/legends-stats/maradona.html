Maradona

Nationality

Argentina

Position

Attacking Midfielder / Forward

About: Diego Maradona, widely known as "El Pibe de Oro" (The Golden Boy), was one of football's most famous and influential players. He was renowned for his extraordinary dribbling, close control, vision, passing, creativity and left-footed technique. His defining achievement came at the 1986 FIFA World Cup, where he captained Argentina to the title, scored 5 goals and provided 5 assists, and won the Golden Ball as the tournament's best player.
