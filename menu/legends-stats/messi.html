Lionel Messi

Nationality

Argentina

Position

Right Winger / Forward / Attacking Midfielder

About: Lionel Messi is widely regarded as one of the greatest footballers in history. Known for his dribbling, close control, vision, passing, playmaking and left-footed finishing, he spent most of his legendary career at Barcelona before moving to PSG and then Inter Miami. He finally achieved his biggest international dream by captaining Argentina to the 2022 FIFA World Cup.
