Zinadine Zidane

Nationality

France

Position

Attacking Midfielder / Central Midfielder

About: Zinedine Zidane is one of football's most iconic midfielders. He was famous for his elegant technique, first touch, dribbling, vision, passing, ball control and ability to control the tempo of a match. He won the 1998 Ballon d'Or, the 1998 World Cup, Euro 2000, and the 2002 Champions League with Real Madrid.
