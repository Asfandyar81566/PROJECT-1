Ronaldinho

Nationality

Brazil

Position

Attacking Midfielder / Left Winger / Forward

About: Ronaldinho is one of football's most entertaining and technically gifted players ever. Famous for his dribbling, flair, skills, creativity, passing, free-kicks and unbelievable close control, he became a global superstar at Barcelona. He won the 2005 Ballon d'Or, the 2006 Champions League, and the 2002 World Cup with Brazil.
