Pele

Nationality

Brazil

Position

Forward / Striker / Attacking Forward

About: Pelé, known as "The King of Football," is one of the most iconic players in football history. He became a global superstar with Santos and Brazil, combining finishing, dribbling, passing, speed, strength, heading and creativity. He won the World Cup at just 17 years old in 1958, then added titles in 1962 and 1970.
